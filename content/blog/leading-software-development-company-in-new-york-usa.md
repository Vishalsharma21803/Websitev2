## Start with the outcome

Before choosing tools or vendors, write down what has to work: who uses the system, which data it touches, and what happens when something goes wrong. That list becomes the brief every later decision is checked against.

## Map the edge cases early

Most projects that stall do so on the cases nobody planned for: missing data, failed integrations, a user who does things in the wrong order. Mapping those before the build starts is cheaper than discovering them in production.

## Plan for life after launch

Monitoring, documentation and a clear owner for fixes are part of the product, not an afterthought. Budget for them from day one.
